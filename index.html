Classify binary & polyatomic compounds into Ionic or Covalent

Ionic Rule: Metal + Nonmetal

Ionic compounds form between a metal (left/center of periodic table) and a nonmetal (or a nonmetal-containing polyatomic ion like NO3−, SO42−, CO32−, OH−, PO43−).

Common Metals: Li, Na, K, Mg, Ca, Sr, Ba, Al, Zn, Ag, Fe, Cu, Pb, Sn, Ni, Co

Covalent Rule: All Nonmetals

Covalent (molecular) compounds consist exclusively of nonmetals sharing electrons. Look for prefixes like di-, tri-, tetra-, penta-, or hexa-.

Nonmetals: H, C, N, O, F, P, S, Cl, Se, Br, I, Xe
